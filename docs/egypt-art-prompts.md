# Egypt illustration prompts

Created with the built-in image generation tool on 12 October 2026. Source photos were used only as private outfit references and are not included in the repository. The panorama is deliberately an imaginative travel game landscape, not geographically exact.

Marco must always be freshly clean-shaven, with no beard, moustache or stubble, in every current and future asset.

Final avatar assets preserve generated transparency, are mechanically trimmed, resized to 500 pixels high and encoded as WebP. The panorama is 1280 pixels wide. Avatar silhouette proportions vary slightly between outfits; they are wardrobe illustrations rather than animation sheets.
