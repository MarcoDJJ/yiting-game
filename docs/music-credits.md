## Audio edits and download provenance

The files are locally served game assets, not remote streaming URLs. Each uses an excerpt from the original recording, with the end blended into its beginning over one four-beat bar based on the author’s published BPM. The edited loops target -20 LUFS and -2 dBTP and are encoded as 112 kbps, 44.1 kHz stereo MP3. Excerpts last 40–82 seconds. Their total audio-file size is 7,877,382 bytes.

`assets/music/catalog.json` records the exact edited duration (`loopDuration`), MP3 container duration (`duration`), source/download URLs, BPM, modifications, byte size and SHA-256 digest for every recording. Container duration includes small encoder padding; `loopDuration` is the intended decoded loop duration.

## Verification

All eight optimized MP3s were probed and decoded completely with FFmpeg without decode errors. Encoding and duration checks verify real stereo audio data rather than download pages or empty files. These technical checks are not a listening review; musical transitions and speaker loudness should also be checked during device playtesting.
